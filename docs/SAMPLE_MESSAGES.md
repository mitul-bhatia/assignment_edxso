# Sample personalized outreach messages

Real drafts generated by the pipeline for real qualified creators (a representative selection of 11 of 111 valid drafts).
Every draft cites a recent public video, is checked by validators (60–90 word email, 15–30 word DM, no claim of having watched
the content, no placeholders), and **awaits human review before any send**. The full set is in
[`data/exports/messages.csv`](../data/exports/messages.csv); contact emails and their sources are in
[`data/exports/influencers.csv`](../data/exports/influencers.csv).

The collaboration angle is chosen by a rule from measured audience features (see the README), not by the model.
